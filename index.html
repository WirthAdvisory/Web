01

Operational Scale-Up

The org that got you to $10M won't get you to $50M. When everyone knows enough of everything and informal coordination still works, the cracks are invisible — until they aren't. Identify where founder-led behaviors have become bottlenecks, build the management layer between you and the business, and create the accountability structures and operating rhythm that let your leadership team run without you in every decision.

Operating Model Management Layer Accountability Leadership Alignment

02

Growth Architecture & Activation

Most growth-stage companies have a market opportunity they're underexploiting — a segment, a vertical, a customer type — where the instinct is right but the model isn't built to win. Diagnose what's actually happening in the market, design the organizational and commercial model to compete there, and activate the motion that turns the opportunity into traction. This is the work that happens before you hire a leader or build a team around an assumption that hasn't been validated.

Market Diagnosis Segment Design Commercial Model Activation

03

Embedded Advisory

Available for select engagements as Fractional COO, Board Member, or Advisory Board Member, depending on what the organization needs and what the moment requires.

04

AI / Applied Intelligence Adoption

Every organization made the shift from typewriters to computers. Not because it was optional — because the ones that didn't got left behind. AI is that moment, right now, for your business. The question isn't whether to adopt it. It's whether your people are actually using it in ways that remove work, raise output quality, and connect to business outcomes — or whether you've bought the tools and called it done. From how your operations run to how you enter new markets, AI belongs in the fabric of how the business works. Not bolted on. Built in.

About

Ross N. Wirth

Wirth Advisory was founded on a straightforward observation: the skills that build a $10M company are not the same skills that scale it to $100M. And most growth-stage CEOs are too close to their business — and too busy running it — to architect the transition themselves.

Ross Wirth brings more than 20 years of operating experience — first as a Managing Director at Accenture, where he led the Insurance practice and was responsible for more than $100M in transformation revenue across the partner practice; then as a Leadership Team member at CyberCube Analytics, a VC and PE-backed company, where he owned more than 75% of company revenue at NRR above 130% and grew the partnership pipeline more than 1,000% annually.

That combination of enterprise rigor and growth-stage reality is what makes the work different. Not strategy theater. Not pure operations. Both — in the right sequence, at the right pace, for where you actually are.
